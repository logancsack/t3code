# The sidebar in Aldo

Aldo's sidebar lists your threads by what each needs from you, rather than by project. Aldo sits at the top: open its conversation, or tap the orb to talk. Below it are the search box and **New thread**.

## Groups

- **Drafts**: threads you started and haven't sent yet.
- **Pinned**: threads you pinned, in the order you arranged them.
- **Waiting on you**: approvals (an email to send, an event to add, a thread Aldo suggests) and threads that ask you something, have a plan to approve, stopped with an error, or have a pull request ready to merge. The one that has waited longest is first.
- **Working**: threads an agent is working on, with the step it's on.
- **Landing**: pull requests that merged and are deploying.
- **Unread**: threads that finished since you last opened them.
- **Earlier**: everything else, folded. Settled threads are always here, whatever they last asked.
- **Snoozed**: threads hidden until they wake, folded.
- **Scheduled**: routines and reminders that will run, folded.

The project menu at the foot narrows the list to one project. A thread counts as settled or snoozed only once its cloud agent says it supports that, so you can always make it active or wake it again from its menu.

## Acting on a thread

Rest the pointer on a thread, or press **Space** on it, to see its latest messages and answer it there. Click it to open it.

Each thread has a menu, the same one as in a thread's header. Open it by right-clicking the thread, choosing **…** on it, or pressing the menu key or **Shift+F10**. On a phone or tablet, touch and hold the thread. From the menu you can pin, settle, snooze, rename, regenerate the title, mark unread, copy its path, branch or ID, open project settings, start a new thread on its branch, archive, or delete it.

Settled threads move to **Earlier**. A thread that's waiting on your answer or approval can't be settled until you answer it or stop it, and Aldo says so without waking its agent.

## Your account

Your picture and name are at the foot. Choose them to see your plan and credits, the settings for your agents, accounts, memory, instructions, vault and appearance, and to manage your sign-in or sign out. **Classic sidebar** there switches to the list of threads by project.
