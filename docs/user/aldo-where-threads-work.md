# Where a thread works in Aldo

Each thread works in one place: **General**, for work outside any project (see [Threads without a repository](./aldo-general-threads.md)), or one of your projects, an Aldo project or one or more repositories. A project's threads share its files, instructions and setup.

## Starting a thread

**New thread** in the sidebar, or its shortcut, opens a thread in the project you're in. From the home screen, or a General thread, it opens in General.

A thread you haven't sent yet shows where it works, large in the middle: its project's picture and name, and what it is. In General it offers the projects you used last, and **Another, or a new project**. Under that, **Recent here** lists the place's latest threads.

The chip at the left of the message box says the same, and picks another place. Whatever you've typed comes along.

The picker lists General first, then your projects, the ones you used last first. You can:

- type to find a project;
- paste a repository's address to work in it;
- type a new name and choose **New project "…"** to make an Aldo project.

On a phone, it opens from the bottom of the screen.

In General, when what you type sounds like one of your projects (you named it, or its threads talk about it), a line above the message box offers **Move it there**.

Until you send it, the thread is **Untitled** in the sidebar and its header. Its first line becomes its title as you type.

On the home screen, the arrow beside **New** picks where a thread starts.

## Moving a thread

Once a thread's first message is sent, its place is set. To move a sent thread:

1. Choose its place at the top of the thread. For a thread in a repository, it's **Move to another project…** in the project's menu.
2. Pick where it should go.

Its agent writes a brief of the conversation: the task, what's decided, what's done and what's left, and the files the new thread should copy over. A new thread opens there with that brief. Once the move is done, the old thread is archived, linked to the new one.

You can also just ask the agent to move the thread.

A sent thread's first line says where it works and when it started.
