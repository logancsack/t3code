# Notifications in Aldo

Aldo can tell your phone or computer when a thread finishes or needs you, even with Aldo closed.

## Turn them on

Open **Settings → General → Notifications** on the device and turn on **Notifications on this device**, then allow notifications when your browser asks. Each device is turned on separately.

- **iPhone and iPad**: notifications work only when Aldo is on your Home Screen. In Safari, choose **Share → Add to Home Screen**, open Aldo from there, and turn them on.
- **Blocked**: if you told your browser to block Aldo's notifications, the switch is off and says so. Allow notifications for Aldo in your browser's site settings, then come back.

## What you're told

You get one notification per thread, replaced by the next one from the same thread:

- **Needs you**: the agent asked you a question, wants an approval, or has a plan for you to review.
- **Done**: the agent finished a turn, with the first line of what it said.
- **Stopped with an error**: the agent's turn failed.

Tap a notification to open the thread. While Aldo is open in front of you, notifications arrive without a sound.

A failed deploy after a merge goes to the agent first; you hear about it when the agent finishes looking into it.

## Turn them off

Turn off **Notifications on this device** in the same place, or block notifications for Aldo in your browser's settings.
